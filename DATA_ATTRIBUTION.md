# Data attribution and modifications

**Houston housing market 2024**, Natasha Lekh (datadetective08), version 1, June 5, 2024 publisher snapshot. Source: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/datadetective08/houston-housing-market-2024 . Publisher-declared license: **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0**, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/ . Changes: light JSON flattened; Texas city labels selected; residential types filtered; invalid/ambiguous records excluded; lot units normalized; ZIP market indicators attached; model outputs computed. No endorsement is implied. Dataset-derived adaptations contributed by this project are made available under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. This is a noncommercial educational research demonstration. Source images and contact fields are not included in app data.

**Zillow Home Value Index**, Zillow Research, https://www.zillow.com/research/data/ . Supplied monthly ZIP mid-tier smoothed seasonally adjusted SFR/condo series through September 2026. The original download date is not known; inspection occurred October 23, 2026. Changes: Texas/city-label filtering, reshaping, historical trend calculations and locally trained forecasts. Retain Zillow attribution with use of the data and derived charts. Do not claim ownership of Zillow's source data or apply the Kaggle publisher's license to unrelated Zillow source material.

**2020 Census ZIP Code Tabulation Areas**, U.S. Census Bureau TIGERweb. Query URL and retrieval timestamp: artifacts/evaluation/boundary_source.json. Returned geometry: WGS84, generalized to 0.0003 degrees. Seven polygon-validity defects from generalization were repaired with Shapely; outside-region property points are hidden from map. ZCTAs approximate postal ZIPs and are not city limits.

Map tiles: OpenStreetMap contributors, as displayed by the map library. No private routing or geocoding service is used. Other rights in underlying source content may remain; the project does not relicense third-party source materials.
